Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Masroof is a personal finance and IOU tracker available at masroof-app.pages.dev. This page explains what data we handle and how. Contact: mahmoudsaad7900@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, and if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture as provided by Google.
  • Data you enter: transactions, categories, friends and IOU entries, and recurring bills you choose to record.
  • Usage statistics: anonymous page-visit counts (device type and language) that are not linked to your account.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your data synced across your devices.
  • To show you your own dashboards, reports and balances.
  • To understand overall app usage (anonymous totals only).

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your financial entries with anyone.

Google user data

When you choose "Continue with Google", we receive only your basic profile (name, email, picture) to create and sign in to your account. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Masroof's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage & security

Data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL). Row-level security ensures each account can only read and change its own records. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

Your choices

  • You can delete all your financial data at any time from Settings → Delete all my data.
  • To delete your account entirely, email mahmoudsaad7900@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.

Terms of Service

  • Masroof is provided free of charge, "as is", as a personal tracking tool. It is not a bank and does not move money.
  • You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you enter and for keeping your sign-in details safe.
  • Don't misuse the service, attempt to access other users' data, or interfere with its operation.
  • We may update the app and these terms; continued use means you accept the current version.
